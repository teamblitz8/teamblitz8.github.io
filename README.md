# Welcome!

This is a website dedicated to Blitz, the STEM Racing team, wholly developed by Chen.
On this website, you will find:
- A fully responsive navigation menu with cool animations on the mobile side when opening and closing it.
- A banner on each page
- A homepage with introduction and summary of what STEM Racing is and who we are.
  - Includes stacks of images which can be flipped by a click or tap next to text
  - A carousel which flicks through cards which explain our journey
  - A model of our car, which is viewed via the model-viewer module by Google
  - A countdown to Regionals powered by JavaScript
  - An embed of some of our Instagram posts using Behold
  - Outline of our awards and achievements
- Introduction of each team member on the about us page
  - Includes a button to expand each team member's card with a cool animation
  - Several reasons why we are valued by our sponsors
- Exploration of our journey
  - A cool timeline built purely on HTML, CSS and JS
- A sponsorship prospectus for prospectus sponsors
  - Embedded onto the contact us page using an embed (contact form coming soon!)
- A place for new teams to gain inspiration through our previous cars.
  - Each car is uploaded as its file and downloadable, and previewed via model-viewer module.

Please contact us if you have any sponsorship enquiries via the email on our prospectus (found on the contact us page).
