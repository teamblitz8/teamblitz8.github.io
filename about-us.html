One team, one mission: an overview of who we are.

We are a competitive STEM Racing Professional Class team of 5 students from St Olave's Grammar School. At Blitz we have over 3 years of experience spanning both regional and national competitions, to which we achieved great success, culminating in our 3rd place finish at the 2024/25 UK National Finals in the Development Class. We are actively seeking financial support to help unlock our final potential for the 2026/27 season.

Meet our members.

Yash Parekh

Team Principal

Yash is currently aspiring to become a mechanical engineer, with a strong interest in aerodynamics and practical problem solving. As not our Team Principal, Yash oversees all team operations and has the final say on everything produced by the team, ensuring it meets the highest of standards, a trait which matches his ambition to lead a project in the future. Outside of his leadership role, Yash also contributes to the car by designing the body and aims to minimise any losses in car performance in order to create the fastest car possible and deliver the best races for the team, specifically cherishing practical experience from STEM Racing.

Chen Hui Wang

Head of Engineering

Chen is keen on developing skills in a wide range of disciplines and thus aspires to study Mechanical Engineering. Within the team his primary role is within car design, specialising specifically within CAA using advanced analysis software such as CFD and FEA, and plays a large role in verifying and maximising the raw performance and potential of our car. Chen also manages the team's social media and aims to maximise the team's outreach. He also designed the website and has a keen interest in programming and coding, skills which he hopes will ensure the team's success on track and the production of a truly creative pit display.

Rayhaan Smith

Head of Enterprise

Rayhaan has a strong interest in aerodynamic vehicles and their operation, and his ambition is to one day become a Commercial Airline Pilot. Being Head of Enterprise, his main functions are to oversee project management and team communication, ensuring work is completed as efficiently as possible whilst holding all work to a high standard, developing our chain of reviews in order to achieve this. As good communication is a critical part of managing an aircraft safely, Rayhaan hopes to develop this skill whilst managing the Enterprise department in the team's shared goal to challenge for podiums with a strong portfolio and Verbal Presentation.

Pranit Malhotra

Design Engineer

Pranit has a keen interest in fluid and thermodynamics and thus is currently aspiring to study Aerospace Engineering. As design engineer, his role specifically is designing the car body. Pranit is also an Arkwright Scholar, having been identified by the Smallpeice Trust as a highly promising young person within engineering and is currently supported by the Dyer's Company. As such Pranit aims to gain valuable practical experience with real-world software, such as Autodesk Fusion and Simscale, and hopes to deliver a car capable of challenging for the top.

Josh Abraham
