A team, led by precision and excellence.

We are a dedicated STEM Racing (formerly F1 in Schools) team from St Olave's Grammar School, consisting of five students united by a passion for STEM and engineering. After achieving 3rd place nationally in the Development Class, we have demonstrated both technical capability and competitive consistency. We are now preparing to enter Professional Class, aiming to further develop our engineering expertise and compete at the highest level.

STEM Racing: From textbook, to track.

STEM Racing is a global competition where students design, manufacture, and race miniature F1-style cars. Formerly known as F1 in Schools, it challenges teams to showcase their skills in every aspect, from aerodynamics to marketing. Participants apply real-world engineering principles, use CAD/CAM/CAE software, whilst also gaining experience in business and finance at regional, national, and international levels.
