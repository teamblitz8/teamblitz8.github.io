An illustrous history.

Team Formation

Nov 2022

St Olave's Grammar School, Orpington


The beginning of our journey, as Team Omega, founded by Yash, Chen, Ifti and Pamoda. From our very first brainstorming sessions came innovative concepts which evolved into key features of our 2023 Regional Finals car, marking the beginning of our engineering journey.

Rebrand

Jan 2023

St Olave's Grammar School, Orpington


Following the departure of two founding members (Ifti and Pamoda), our team welcomed the new talent of Pranit and Rayhaan, bringing fresh ideas and expertise to build on the foundations already established. It marked the beginning of a new chapter, with our rebrand to Blitz reflecting our renewed vision and commitment.

London North Regional Finals

Dec 2023

Harlow College, Harlow


We competed in our first ever F1 in Schools (STEM Racing) competition at the London North Regional Finals, where months of dedication, innovation, and teamwork came to fruition and recognised with a 2nd place finish in the Entry Class, alongside winning two prestigious awards across all classes.

New Members

Jan 2024

St Olave's Grammar School, Orpington


Following our first competition we introduce two new members to the team: Josh and Anu as we sought to strengthen our Enterprise team and begin preparations for the novel challenge of Development Class and the additional work needed for it.

London South Regional Finals

Jan 2025

Canterbury Christ Church University, Canterbury


We attended our first Development Class Regional Finals competition with great success, securing qualification to the National Finals with a 2nd place finish along with multiple awards. It also provided us with critical experience in newer areas such as the Enterprise Portfolio, allowing us to bring our best to the National Finals.

UK National Finals

Mar 2025

Magna Science Adventure Centre, Rotherham


Our engineering vision and passion culminates in our first ever trip to the National Finals. A competitive car, pit display and consistent results across the board earn us a podium nationally for the Development Class.
