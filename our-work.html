Our way of giving back.

Starting your STEM Racing journey without any prior experience or mentorship can be tough alone. As a team, we have decided to open source all competition materials we have previously submitted, to support new teams who are looking for a place to start in the competition. Explore our competition-ready cars, their drag values and the real track times that they produced at the competition.

Originality

The work showcased on this website is intended to inspire and guide new STEM Racing teams. While you're welcome to learn from our approaches and use them to develop your own ideas, please do not copy our designs, content, or work directly. Every team should aim to develop its own original solutions, creativity, and engineering identity.
